## The three sentences

You should be able to say these out loud in under 30 seconds.

1. **The problem:** "Wildfire money goes out every year, but [who] cannot easily see which ready projects are still waiting, or which high-risk places nobody has funded."
2. **What we do:** "We keep one checked record of projects and money, and we introduce a funder to a project only after both agree."
3. **Why it is better than a spreadsheet:** "Every row shows where it came from and whether a person has checked it, so you can trust it and move quickly."

Test them on someone who has never heard of the project. If they ask "so what do you do?" the sentences need work.
