# Calls with communities (20 minutes)

Communities means HOA leaders, Firewise communities, fire districts, neighborhood groups and small nonprofits doing wildfire work.

## Before the call
Know their county and whether they have applied for anything recently.

## Questions, in order

**1. Their story (5 min)**
- Tell me about the last time your group tried to get money for wildfire work. What happened?
- Who did the work of applying? How many hours did it take?
- What happened after you applied?

**2. How they find money (5 min)**
- How do you hear about programs?
- What do you do when a program's rules are confusing?
- Have you ever given up on an application? Why?

**3. Size of the ask (4 min)**
- How would you decide how much to ask for?
- What numbers do you have already? (homes, acres, a plan?)
- What do you not know?

**4. Privacy and trust (3 min)**
- If we listed your project publicly, what would you want shown and what kept private?
- What would make you comfortable giving us your email?

**5. Close (3 min)**
- What one thing would have saved you the most time last time?
- Who else in your area should I talk to?

## Do not ask
- Anything with the words "platform", "dashboard" or "data."
- "Would you fill out a form?" Watch what they say about forms they already hate.
