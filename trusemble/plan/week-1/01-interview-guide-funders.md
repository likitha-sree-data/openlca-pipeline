# Calls with funders (20 minutes)

Funders means foundations, donors, public programs and investors who give or lend money for wildfire work.

## Before the call
Read their website for 5 minutes. Know what they have funded in the last two years.

## Questions, in order

**1. How they work today (5 min)**
- Walk me through the last project you decided to fund. How did you first hear about it?
- Where do you look when you want to find new projects?
- Who else is involved in the decision?

**2. What "ready" means (5 min)**
- What does a project need before you will seriously consider it?
- What makes you say no quickly?
- What do you wish you could see on one page about a project?

**3. Trust (4 min)**
- If a record said "from a public record, not yet checked by a person," would you use it? What would you do next?
- What would make you trust a number or a claim you saw here?

**4. Getting introduced (3 min)**
- How do you like to be introduced to a project or a community?
- What would put you off?

**5. Close (3 min)**
- If this tool existed and worked perfectly, how would your week change?
- Who else should I talk to?

## Do not ask
- "Would you use this?" People say yes to be polite.
- "Do you like the map?" You haven't shown it.
