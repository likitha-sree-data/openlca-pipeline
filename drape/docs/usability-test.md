# Drape usability test kit

For observed sessions, in person or over a video call. Each session takes about 40 minutes. Run it with study mode on if you want task timings recorded (Study participation page, Session timers), or keep times by hand on the log sheet.

## Before the session

- Device: the participant's own phone if possible (Drape is mobile first), or a laptop at 1280px wide.
- Account: a fresh account for setup tasks, or the demo (Try the demo) for tasks 2 to 4 if the participant does not want to photograph their clothes.
- Props: 4 to 6 of the participant's own garments, a plain bed or floor, and one outfit they are wearing.
- Consent: the study consent screen in Drape is a placeholder. Use your IRB-approved consent before recruiting anyone.
- Say: "We are testing the app, not you. Think aloud. There are no wrong answers."

## Tasks

### 1. Setup

"Add three pieces of your clothing to Drape."

Success: three garments saved with the right category and a recognisable name.

Metrics to note:
- time to first saved garment, and to all three
- number of photos retaken
- fields corrected on Review details (category, color, name)
- whether the person noticed the Check details dot or the cutout tip
- errors or hesitations at the camera or file picker

### 2. Outfit choices

"Find an outfit for a work meeting tomorrow and plan it for that day. Then swap the shoes for another pair."

Success: an outfit planned for tomorrow with the Meeting occasion, shoes swapped.

Metrics to note:
- time to plan
- whether the person found swapping by tapping a piece
- whether they read the reasons
- use of More outfits from these pieces, Compare, or Ask
- comments on whether suggestions feel like their own style

### 3. Purchase review

"Here is a product you might like (give a link or a screenshot). Use Drape to decide what to do."

Success: the person reaches a decision, Save for 48 hours or Keep browsing.

Metrics to note:
- time on the Review screen before deciding
- whether they looked at Already in your wardrobe and Works with
- the decision, and the reasons they say out loud
- whether either button felt pushed or preferred
- any sign of pressure, guilt or annoyance

### 4. Photo matching

"Take a photo of what you are wearing today and record it."

Success: matches confirmed and wear counts updated.

Metrics to note:
- number of matches the person changed
- whether low-confidence matches (Please check) were noticed
- whether they understood that nothing counts until they confirm
- whether they changed Keep my original photo, and why
- comfort with photographing themselves (1 to 5, ask after)

## After the session

Ask, on a 1 to 7 scale where 7 is best:
- How easy was Drape to use?
- How much did Drape help you use clothes you already own?
- How comfortable were you with the photos Drape keeps?

Then two open questions:
- What would make you open Drape again next week?
- Was there a moment you felt pushed? Where?
