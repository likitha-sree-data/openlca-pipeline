# Taking part in the Drape study

**What the study is about.** We want to learn whether seeing similar clothes you already own changes a decision about buying something new.

**What you will do.** Use Drape as you normally would. When you check an item before buying it, you may or may not see information from your wardrobe. After you choose, we ask a few short questions. About 48 hours later we ask whether you bought the item.

**What we record.** Your choice, your reasons, how sure you felt, how long you looked at the page, and your answer 48 hours later. These are stored under a code, not your name or email. We never include photos in study data.

**Your choices.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time from Account and settings, and you can ask us to delete your study data when you stop. Stopping does not change how Drape works for you.
